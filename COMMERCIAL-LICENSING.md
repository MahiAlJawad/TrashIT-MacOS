The source in this repository is offered under **GPL-3.0-or-later**. The copyright holder may also offer the same copyrightable work under a separate commercial license.

A purchased, signed TrashIT GUI build is expected to be distributed under a commercial EULA supplied with that build. Purchasing a build pays for trusted signing, notarization, updates, and support; it does not take away anyone's GPL rights to source or GPL builds already received. Public GPL builds must remain functional without an App Store receipt or commercial key.

No commercial license or EULA is granted by this file. Before a paid release, project counsel must approve the commercial license, direct-sale EULA, App Store custom EULA, contributor agreement, and dependency policy.
